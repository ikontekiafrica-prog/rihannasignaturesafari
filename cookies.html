Interactive Cookie Preference Center

You retain total authority over the cookies and local storage tokens utilized during your visit to Rihanna Signature Safari. Choose your preferred privacy posture below; changes take effect immediately and are remembered across sessions:

01. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are minute alphanumeric text files placed onto your computer, tablet, or smartphone when you access digital websites. They allow web platforms to recognize your browser, remember preferred settings, and deliver essential site functionality between page requests.

Alongside standard HTTP cookies, modern web platforms may utilize browser Local Storage (an HTML5 storage mechanism). Local storage stores data locally within your browser without sending it automatically with every server header, resulting in faster load times and higher privacy security.

02. How Rihanna Signature Safari Uses Cookies

We deploy cookies and local storage tokens solely to provide an elegant, dependable digital experience that mirrors the luxury of our private safaris. We use them specifically to:

  • Preserve your cookie consent selections across page visits without repeatedly bothering you with prompts.
  • Maintain secure administrative sessions within our private portal.
  • Enable interactive features, such as currency filters, destination previews, and inquiry forms.
  • Analyze aggregated, non-personally identifiable navigation metrics to identify slow-loading pages and enhance user experience.

03. Categories of Cookies Deployed

We group the cookies and storage technologies used on our digital platforms into three clear categories:

Category Purpose & Description Duration
Strictly Necessary Essential for website navigation, CSRF protection, and core technical security. The site cannot function properly without these tokens. Cannot be disabled. Session / 1 Year
Functional & Preferences Stores your cookie consent choice (rihanna_cookie_consent) and active itinerary filters so your preferences persist across visits. 12 Months
Analytics & Performance Gathers anonymous, privacy-friendly data on page load latencies, scroll depth, and interaction counts to help us optimize server performance. 24 Months

04. Third-Party Technologies & Content Services

To render typography and interactive maps of East Africa with maximum elegance, we link to trusted global infrastructure providers:

  • Google Fonts: Typography stylesheets for high-definition luxury serifs (Cormorant Garamond and Playfair Display) are served via Google's edge cache. No user tracking is performed by these style assets.
  • Font Awesome: Scalable vector icons illustrating safari amenities, wildlife categories, and contact channels.
  • Interactive Wilderness Maps: Map previews powered by Leaflet and OpenStreetMap open-source mapping libraries, which operate under strict privacy-first open standards.

05. Controlling Cookies Through Your Web Browser

In addition to using our interactive Preference Center above, most modern web browsers allow you to manage cookie behavior directly through browser settings:

  • Apple Safari (macOS & iOS): Preferences → Privacy → Prevent cross-site tracking and Block all cookies.
  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.
Note Regarding Blocking Essential Cookies

Disabling strictly necessary cookies in your browser may prevent our reservation inquiry forms and interactive itineraries from submitting properly.
