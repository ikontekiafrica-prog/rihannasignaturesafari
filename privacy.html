Data Discretion Guarantee

We hold client itineraries, guest identities, and travel schedules in absolute confidentiality. We never sell, monetize, or disclose your personal data to unauthorized third parties.

Last Updated: November 2026 Tanzania PDPA 2022 Compliant High-Level Encryption

01. Preamble & Privacy Commitment

At Rihanna Signature Safari Ltd. ("Rihanna Signature Safari," "we," "our," or "us"), safeguarding the trust, privacy, and personal data of our esteemed travelers is foundational to our philosophy of ultra-luxury bespoke hospitality.

This Privacy Policy outlines the standards, measures, and protocols governing how we collect, process, utilize, secure, and disclose your personal details when you visit our website (rihannasignaturesafari.com), request a tailor-made safari proposal, or embark on a guided wildlife expedition across Tanzania and Kenya.

Statutory Compliance Standard

Our operational practices comply with the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), 2022, international privacy regulations including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for our global guests, and relevant aviation and tourism sector directives.

02. Categories of Personal Information We Collect

Curating a seamless, ultra-luxury African expedition demands meticulous planning. To execute your bespoke itinerary with total precision and security, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal & Contact Identifiers: Full legal names (as shown on passport), nationality, residential country, email address, direct telephone and WhatsApp contact details.
  • Travel & Passport Credentials: Passport numbers, issue and expiry dates, country of issuance, and dates of birth. These are strictly required by the Tanzania National Parks Authority (TANAPA), Ngorongoro Conservation Area Authority (NCAA), Kenya Wildlife Service (KWS), and civil aviation authorities to issue mandatory park permits and bush flight passenger manifests.
  • Health, Dietary & Medical Safeguards: Food allergies, dietary preferences, physical mobility requirements, and emergency medical contacts. Crucially, blood group or pre-existing medical conditions may be requested solely for registration with AMREF Flying Doctors for emergency evacuation insurance.
  • Flight & Transfer Coordinates: Commercial international flight numbers, arrival and departure schedules, and private aircraft tail numbers to coordinate VIP tarmac meets and chartered transfers.
  • Digital Interactions & Technical Identifiers: IP addresses, browser types, device information, and pages viewed during visits to our digital portal, utilized solely to ensure seamless portal performance and security.

03. Lawful Grounds & Purposes of Processing

Rihanna Signature Safari processes your personal data strictly on lawful grounds recognized by international privacy laws and Tanzanian statutes:

  1. Performance of a Contract: Processing is indispensable to prepare and deliver the safari itinerary you have commissioned, confirm private lodge reservations, schedule bush charters, and procure wildlife entry permits in your name.
  2. Compliance with Legal & Regulatory Obligations: We are legally mandated to submit passenger manifests to the Tanzania Civil Aviation Authority (TCAA), maintain accurate guest registries for tax compliance under the Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA), and register visitors with conservation bodies.
  3. Vital Interests & Emergency Medical Care: Processing medical details to facilitate rapid 24/7 medical evacuation and hospital dispatch through AMREF Flying Doctors in the event of unforeseen trauma or illness in remote bush areas.
  4. Legitimate Interests: Enhancing our website functionality, safeguarding our systems against cyber vulnerabilities, and understanding guest preferences to continuously elevate our service standards.

04. Strict Non-Disclosure & Permitted Third-Party Sharing

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data. Your information is treated as privileged hospitality data. Third-party disclosure is restricted exclusively to vetted entities essential for your journey:

  • Conservation & Park Authorities: TANAPA, NCAA, KWS, and wildlife management areas for entry passes, ranger escorts, and conservation permits.
  • Aviation Charter Operators: Licensed domestic carriers (e.g., Coastal Aviation, Auric Air, Safari Air Link, or private charter providers) requiring precise passenger weights and passport records for flight manifests.
  • Luxury Bush Lodges & Tented Camps: Exclusive properties where you will reside, strictly for room allocation, personalized hospitality, and dietary compliance.
  • Emergency Medical Providers: AMREF Flying Doctors and authorized regional emergency clinics in Nairobi and Dar es Salaam for life-safety services.
  • Financial & Banking Partners: Regulated international payment processors handling credit card verification and authorized SWIFT bank wires.

05. International Data Transfers

Because Rihanna Signature Safari hosts discerning travelers from North America, Europe, the Middle East, Asia, and Australasia, personal data may be transmitted across international boundaries between your home country and our operations center in Tanzania.

When transmitting data internationally, we employ robust data security measures, encrypted transfer protocols, and contractual covenants ensuring that your personal data receives a level of protection fully equivalent to international standards.

06. Security Safeguards & Data Protection

We deploy modern technical and administrative safeguards to protect your personal details against accidental destruction, unauthorized disclosure, theft, and illicit tampering:

  • Encryption: All digital communications via our portal are encrypted using 256-bit Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) certificates.
  • Restricted Staff Access: Only dedicated safari architects and operations managers with verified clearance have access to sensitive guest dossiers and passport records.
  • Secure Storage: Digital records reside on hardened cloud and local databases protected by multi-factor authentication and state-of-the-art intrusion prevention systems.

07. Data Retention Schedules

We retain your personal data only for as long as is strictly necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was gathered, including satisfying legal, accounting, tax, or reporting obligations:

  • Safari Dossiers & Financial Accounting: Maintained for a period of seven (7) years following journey completion in accordance with Tanzanian commercial and tax regulations.
  • Passport Copies & Permits: Retained for the duration of the booking cycle through post-trip support, after which digital passport scans are permanently purged from active operations systems.
  • General Inquiries: Unconfirmed custom trip inquiries are archived or removed after twenty-four (24) months if no booking is formalized.

08. Your Guest Privacy Rights

Regardless of your country of origin, Rihanna Signature Safari respects your statutory privacy entitlements under applicable data protection legislation, including:

  • Right to Access: You may request a formal copy of the personal information we hold regarding your travel party.
  • Right to Rectification: You are entitled to correct any outdated, inaccurate, or incomplete information promptly.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the deletion of your personal data when it is no longer required for contractual or statutory obligations.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: You may restrict certain processing activities or opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

To exercise any of these privileges, please send a written request to our Data Privacy Officer at info@rihannasignaturesafari.com.

09. Cookies & Digital Tracking

Our website utilizes cookies and similar local storage technologies to guarantee smooth navigation, remember your device preferences, and evaluate website traffic to refine our digital experience.

You can configure your browser to decline non-essential cookies or customize your preferences directly on our interactive Cookie Policy Page at any time.
