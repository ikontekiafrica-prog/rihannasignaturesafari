01. Preamble & Scope of Agreement

Welcome to Rihanna Signature Safari Ltd. ("Company," "we," "our," or "us"). These Terms and Conditions constitute a legally binding agreement between Rihanna Signature Safari Ltd. and the primary traveler and all members of their travel party ("Guest," "Client," or "you").

By requesting a customized safari dossier, paying a deposit, or participating in any safari, mountain climbing, island retreat, or private aviation charter operated or arranged by Rihanna Signature Safari, you explicitly acknowledge, accept, and agree to be bound by all terms, provisions, and conditions outlined herein.

02. Tailor-Made Reservations, Deposits & Payment Schedules

Because all Rihanna Signature Safari expeditions are 100% private and curated bespoke, lodging accommodations, private sky charters, and national park permits must be reserved in advance through formal contractual allocations.

  • Initial Reservation Deposit: A non-refundable commitment deposit of 30% of the total expedition cost is required upon itinerary confirmation to secure premier lodge suites, private bush flights, and regulatory permits.
  • Final Balance Due: The remaining 70% balance must be cleared no later than 60 days prior to your scheduled arrival date in Tanzania or Kenya.
  • Late Bookings: For reservations confirmed within 60 days of arrival, 100% of the total expedition cost is payable immediately upon receipt of confirmation.

Payment Channels & Currencies

All quotations are calculated in United States Dollars (USD). Payments are accepted via direct international SWIFT bank wire transfers directly to our corporate commercial bank accounts in Dar es Salaam, or through certified, secure electronic credit card portals (Visa, MasterCard, American Express).

03. Cancellation, Postponement & Refund Policy

All cancellation notices must be delivered in writing via electronic mail directly to our reservations desk at info@rihannasignaturesafari.com. Cancellations take effect on the working date written confirmation is acknowledged.

  • 61 Days or More Prior to Arrival: The 30% commitment deposit is retained. However, in qualifying circumstances, deposits (less incurred permit and aviation fees) may be credited toward a rescheduled safari within twelve (12) calendar months.
  • 60 to 31 Days Prior to Arrival: A cancellation charge of 50% of the total expedition value is assessed.
  • 30 to 0 Days Prior to Arrival (or No-Show): A cancellation charge of 100% of the total expedition value is assessed.

Non-Refundable Third-Party Allocations: Gorilla trekking permits, chimpanzee tracking permits in Mahale Mountains, private charter flight legs, and non-refundable lodge guarantees (including Singita, &Beyond, and Four Seasons) are governed strictly by supplier policies and cannot be refunded once issued.

04. Medical Coverage & AMREF Flying Doctors Evacuation

The health and well-being of our guests is paramount. While our field teams maintain rigorous wilderness medical training and carry comprehensive trauma kits, all guests are required to maintain valid international medical and emergency travel insurance.

Complimentary AMREF Flying Doctors Air Evacuation

As a hallmark of Rihanna Signature Safari hospitality, every traveler on our private itineraries is enrolled in AMREF Flying Doctors 24/7 Emergency Medical Air Evacuation coverage throughout Tanzania and Kenya. In the unlikely event of acute trauma or severe illness, intensive-care-equipped aircraft and medical flight crews evacuate guests from remote wilderness airstrips directly to world-standard medical centers in Nairobi or Dar es Salaam.

05. Passports, East African Visas & Health Protocols

Guests bear sole responsibility for ensuring all international travel documents and inoculations meet the sovereign requirements of the United Republic of Tanzania and the Republic of Kenya:

  • Passport Validity: All passports must possess a minimum validity of six (6) months beyond your planned date of departure and hold at least three (3) consecutive blank visa pages.
  • Tourist Visas: Guests are strongly encouraged to obtain their Tanzanian and Kenyan electronic tourist visas (e-Visa) via official government portals prior to departure.
  • Yellow Fever Certification: An international certificate of vaccination against Yellow Fever is mandatory if arriving from or transiting through endemic nations.
  • Malaria Prophylaxis: East Africa is a malaria-endemic region. Guests are advised to consult their personal physician or travel clinic for appropriate preventative medications.

06. Domestic Flight Baggage Restrictions (Bush Charters)

Private bush aviation (Cessna Grand Caravan and King Air aircraft) operating between remote dirt airstrips enforce strict air-safety weight and dimension boundaries:

Strict 15 kg (33 lbs) Duffel Bag Allowance

Luggage is restricted to a maximum of 15 kilograms (33 lbs) per passenger, inclusive of personal hand baggage and camera equipment. Baggage must be packed in soft-sided duffel bags without rigid external frames or hard wheels to allow safe stowing inside internal aircraft cargo pods. Secure luggage storage is available at our Dar es Salaam or Arusha base for excess baggage not needed in the bush.

07. Wildlife Safety, Park Regulations & Guide Authority

East Africa’s national parks and conservation areas are unfenced, pristine wilderness ecosystems inhabited by free-roaming wild fauna. Your private Tanzanian and Kenyan naturalist guides possess gold-standard certifications and deep tracking mastery.

  • Guests must adhere unequivocally to guide directives, game-drive safety protocols, and camp walking regulations at all times.
  • Under Tanzania National Parks (TANAPA) and Kenya Wildlife Service (KWS) law, feeding wildlife, littering, making excessive noise, or alighting from safari vehicles outside designated picnic clearings is strictly forbidden.
  • Walking safaris and night game drives are conducted exclusively in licensed conservancies accompanied by armed wildlife rangers.

08. Force Majeure & Itinerary Flexibility

Rihanna Signature Safari reserves the right to adapt or reroute an itinerary in the event of unforeseen natural conditions, heavy seasonal rains causing bridge or runway closures, sudden park authority restrictions, civil unrest, or unseasonal wildlife migrations. In such instances, our expedition dispatch will substitute accommodations and air routes of equal or superior standard.

While we exercise the utmost diligence in selecting lodges, chartered air carriers, and private vessels, we cannot accept liability for personal injury, loss, property damage, or delays caused by third-party suppliers or sovereign acts beyond our direct control.

This agreement and any disputes arising under or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the United Republic of Tanzania. Any legal action or proceeding shall be brought exclusively in the competent courts of Dar es Salaam, Tanzania.
